What do the status codes mean?

These indicator categories describe the frequency with which a plant occurs in wetlands versus non-wetlands.

OBL
Obligate Wetland

Almost always occurs in wetlands.

FACW
Facultative Wetland

Usually occurs in wetlands, but may occur in non-wetlands.

FAC
Facultative

Occurs in wetlands and non-wetlands.

FACU
Facultative Upland

Usually occurs in non-wetlands, but may occur in wetlands.

UPL
Obligate Upland

Almost always occurs in non-wetlands.

U: The letter U appears in the supplied source data. It is not one of the five standard indicator categories shown above; its source-specific meaning is not inferred here.

Indicator status is one piece of vegetation evidence. It does not determine whether a site is a wetland by itself. Consult the applicable regional list and regulatory guidance for project decisions.

Use the selected source’s rating as printed.

USACE and SFWMD ratings are kept separate and should not be treated as interchangeable.
