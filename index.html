What do the status codes mean?

These indicator categories describe how frequently a plant occurs in wetlands versus non-wetlands.

OBL — Obligate Wetland: Almost always occurs in wetlands.

FACW — Facultative Wetland: Usually occurs in wetlands, but may occur in non-wetlands.

FAC — Facultative: Occurs in wetlands and non-wetlands.

FACU — Facultative Upland: Usually occurs in non-wetlands, but may occur in wetlands.

UPL — Obligate Upland: Almost always occurs in non-wetlands.

U: This notation appears in the supplied source data. It is not one of the five standard indicator categories above; its source-specific meaning is not inferred here.

Indicator status is one piece of vegetation evidence. It does not determine whether a site is a wetland by itself. Consult the applicable regional list and regulatory guidance for project decisions.
